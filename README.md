🎯 Educational Philosophy

My goal is to create learning experiences that move students beyond simply memorizing code.

Students should understand:

What they are doing
↓
Why they are doing it
↓
How it works
↓
How to apply it
↓
How to create something of their own

Programming is most meaningful when students can use it to solve problems, experiment, create, and communicate ideas.

🧭 Learning Model

The curriculum follows a simple progression:

1. 📖 Lessons

Learn a new concept through explanations, examples, demonstrations, and guided activities.

2. 🧪 Practice

Reinforce the concept through exercises and smaller challenges.

3. 🚀 Projects

Combine multiple concepts to create something meaningful.

This approach helps students gradually move from:

Understanding → Practicing → Applying → Creating
